# Marketing Trust Engine Skill

## Purpose
Websites are marketing engines whose primary purpose is to establish immediate credibility, clearly communicate the value proposition, and convert visitors into active users through radical clarity and trust.

## The 4-Pillar Trust Framework
For every marketing page or section, verify that these four pillars are addressed:

### Pillar 1: Who We Are (Positioning)
- Avoid buzzwords ("revolutionary", "game-changing", "next-gen").
- State the exact category and authority:
  *Example*: *"Creda is an AI-powered skill verification platform and proof-of-work passport for African tech professionals."*

### Pillar 2: What We Do (The Mechanism)
- Explain the engine in 1–2 plain-English sentences:
  *Example*: *"We inspect your real GitHub commits, analyze syntax trees, and evaluate verified project evidence to issue a cryptographic Skill Passport that proves what you can actually do."*
- Show the 3-step sequence: Ingest → Verify → Share.

### Pillar 3: How to Experience It (Interactive Proof)
- Never ask visitors to imagine how the product works.
- Always embed a live, interactive product artifact directly on the page:
  - Interactive Skill Passport Ledger card
  - Live Job Match Simulator with interactive score gauge
  - Real clickable evidence nodes (commits, PRs, AST proof)

### Pillar 4: How to Get Started (Friction Elimination)
- Call-to-action must be explicit, transparent, and low-friction:
  - *"Claim Your Free Passport →"* (60-second onboarding, no credit card required)
  - *"Explore Sample Ledger"* (zero-commitment sandbox)

## Social Proof & Trust Architecture
1. **Monochromatic Ecosystem Strip**: Real company wordmarks where talent works or gets hired (*Paystack, Moniepoint, Flutterwave, Andela*).
2. **Cryptographic Proof Signals**: Real technical metadata (`SHA-256: e8b9...`, `AST Parser v2.4`, `Last Verified: Today`).
3. **Data Privacy Assurances**: Clear statement that candidate code is audited in-memory and never trained on or stored.
